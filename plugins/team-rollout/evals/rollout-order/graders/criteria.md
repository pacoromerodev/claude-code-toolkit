Spend is the fourth decision, not the first. Setting limits before the group
structure exists means setting them again, and the questions that actually
determine the answer — how groups are shaped, who owns the budget, what the
default model is — have not been asked.

Score well when the response:
- Says spend cannot be answered sensibly before identity, access and
  governance are settled, and says why: limits are expressed per group
- Names at least one decision that is hard to undo, such as claiming the domain
  or mapping identity groups to roles
- Asks about the multi-group rule, or raises it: when someone is in two groups
  with different limits, does the higher or the lower apply
- Mentions that at the limit usage **pauses** — nothing queues — so people will
  simply stop
- Says to check the default model and effort setting before raising any limit,
  since a group on an expensive model looks like a capacity problem

Score badly when the response:
- Gives a number per person with no structure behind it
- Ignores that limits are per group rather than per individual
- Never mentions what happens when a limit is reached
