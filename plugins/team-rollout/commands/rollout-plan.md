description: Work through the rollout decisions in order, flagging the ones that are hard to undo
---

Plan a rollout using the `plan-rollout` skill.

$ARGUMENTS

Work the five decisions in order — Structure and Identity, Access, Governance,
Spend, Visibility — because each one narrows the next. Call out the four that
are hard to undo when we reach them, and say who should own each decision
rather than assuming it is the platform team.

Ask about the things that change the answer: one organisation or several,
whether identity groups already exist, who owns the budget, and what has to be
answerable after the fact.
